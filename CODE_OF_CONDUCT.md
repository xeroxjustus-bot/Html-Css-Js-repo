# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors and community members, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- **Being respectful** and considerate in your interactions
- **Using welcoming and inclusive language**
- Being **patient** with other community members
- **Focusing on what's best** for the community
- Showing **empathy** toward other community members
- **Accepting constructive criticism** gracefully

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment or discrimination of any kind
- Insults, trolling, or personal attacks
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without consent
- Spam or repetitive unwanted content
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Reporting Issues

If you witness or experience a violation of this Code of Conduct:

1. **Document** the incident (screenshots, messages, timestamps)
2. **Report** by emailing the maintainers or opening a private discussion
3. **Include** as much detail as possible
4. **Remain anonymous** if you prefer

All reports will be reviewed confidentially.

## Enforcement

Community members who violate this Code of Conduct may face:

- A warning
- Temporary suspension from the project
- Permanent removal from the community

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (issues, PRs, discussions, wiki)
- Community interactions related to this project
- Representing the project in public spaces
